This is TasteWeave, a taste graph planner built for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon. You start in the profile studio by rating attributes across music, film, food, books, and places. The similarity engine then explains every match it shows you, attribute by attribute, so a recommendation is never a black box. The afternoon planner is where the agent earns its name. Set a budget and your mood, and the planner assembles a complete culture session, something to listen to, watch, cook, and read, while the agent log narrates each decision in seven phases. When money runs short it downgrades gracefully and says why. Your profile becomes a live signature, a radar chart and a woven thread drawn on canvas that redraws with every slider move. The dataset is clearly synthetic, and the two seams where the Qloo taste API plugs in are documented for judges.
